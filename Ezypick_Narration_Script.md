# Ezypick — 2-Minute Pitch Narration Script

**Target:** ~2:00 total (this draft ≈ 1:55, ~280 words). Speak at a calm pace (~145 wpm), pause briefly on each new slide.
**Tip:** the times are a guide — practise once with a timer and trim the longest lines if you run over.

---

**Slide 1 — Cover  [0:00–0:08]**
> "Hi, I'm [your name] — this is Ezypick, and here's how I designed it, from a big idea to the first screens."

**Slide 2 — Big Idea  [0:08–0:19]**
> "We make dozens of small decisions a day, and they quietly wear us out. One of the most common? Deciding where to eat. So my big idea is decision fatigue."

**Slide 3 — Essential Question  [0:19–0:31]**
> "That led to my essential question: how might we take the stress out of deciding where to eat? It sounds simple — but it depends on so many things at once, and it's even harder in a group."

**Slide 4 — The Challenge  [0:31–0:39]**
> "So my challenge: take all those factors — cuisine, budget, timing, the weather — and turn them into a few simple yes-or-no questions."

**Slide 5 — Domain Investigation  [0:39–0:49]**
> "To dig in, I asked friends how they really choose. Alone, they default to the same few spots; in a group, they just go round in circles."

**Slide 6 — Meet Alex  [0:49–1:00]**
> "Like Alex — 23, working in the city, surrounded by restaurants, yet always the same takeaway, because it's easier. And with colleagues, no one wants to be the one to pick."

**Slide 7 — Problem & Opportunity  [1:00–1:09]**
> "So the problem is clear: too many options, no decision. The opportunity — turn 'where should we eat?' into one confident choice, solo or together."

**Slide 8 — Solution Ideas  [1:09–1:17]**
> "I brainstormed a few approaches: smart filters, a random pick, map browsing, or swiping yes-or-no on cuisine and vibe."

**Slide 9 — Decision Matrix  [1:17–1:26]**
> "I scored them on effort, speed and group-friendliness. Swiping won clearly — the only one that's fast, effortless, and works for a whole group."

**Slide 10 — From Idea to Prototype  [1:26–1:35]**
> "To build it, I paired each key question with an activity — from testing the questions on friends to a clickable prototype."

**Slide 11 — The App: Ezypick  [1:35–1:52]**
> "And that's Ezypick. A few swipes on cuisine and vibe, and it picks one restaurant nearby — on your own, or matched across a group. No scrolling, no ordering. Here are the first screens: pick a mode, swipe, and get your spot."

**Slide 12 — Thank You  [1:52–1:56]**
> "Ezypick — one gesture, one decision. Thanks for watching!"

---

*Total ≈ 1:55. If you land under 2:00 you're golden; if you creep over, the easiest trims are Slide 3 and Slide 11.*
