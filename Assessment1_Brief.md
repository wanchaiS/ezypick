**App Design Development Process Walkthrough Video**

---

## Assessment Description

For this individual assessment, you are required to create a **2-minute video** that guides the viewer through the key stages of the app design development process. Your video should cover the journey from the **"Big Idea" generation** to **sketching the initial app interface (Lo-Fi)**.

This assessment aims to evaluate your:

- Understanding of the app design process
- Ability to communicate complex concepts concisely
- Creative thinking in translating ideas into visual representations

You will demonstrate your problem-solving and creativity skills by presenting your proposed app design solution. The assessment focuses on designing and evaluating the viability and value of the presented solution, as well as assessing the clarity and effectiveness of the idea in addressing the given problem.

---

## Individual Assessment Components

**1. Big Idea**
Each student will present their individual big idea.

**2. Essential Questioning**
Choose one essential question you formulated during the activity and present it along with your rationale. Demonstrate critical thinking and the ability to focus on the core problem the app aims to solve.

**3. Challenge Creation — Challenge Mindmapping**
Select a challenge statement you contributed to and explain how it aligns with the essential question. Discuss the significance of this challenge statement and how it shapes the direction of your app design solution, using mind mapping to visually represent your thought process.

**4. Domain Investigation**
For a simulated domain investigation task, formulate guiding questions and conduct research.

**5. Domain Personae**
Create a domain persona based on specific user characteristics. This evaluates your ability to understand user needs.

**6. Problem/Opportunity Statement**
Craft a problem/opportunity statement for a given challenge. Demonstrate your ability to synthesise research and articulate a focused challenge.

**7. Generating Solution Concepts — Mind Mapping**
Create a visual mind map of different solution ideas and how they connect to address the app's problem statement (see Appendix A).

**8. Selecting ONE Solution Concept — Decision Matrix**
Evaluate and rank a set of solution concepts (generated through mind mapping) using a decision matrix. This assesses your analytical skills and ability to justify your choices.

**9. Domain/Applied Investigation — Guiding Questions & Activities**
Create a list of guiding questions and corresponding activities for transforming a solution concept into a prototype. This evaluates your ability to plan the execution of a solution.

**10. App Statement & Sketch**
Create an app statement and a simple sketch of the user interface for a selected solution concept. This tests your ability to communicate design ideas clearly.

> Overall, this assessment encourages you to think critically, collaborate effectively, and showcase your creativity and problem-solving skills throughout the app design and development process. The final pitch presentation/video allows you to articulate and defend your proposed solution effectively, highlighting its relevance, potential benefits, and effectiveness in addressing the given problem.

---

## Instructions

### 1. Content

Your video should cover the following stages of the app design development process:

- **Big Idea** — State your app big idea.
- **Essential Questioning** — Describe the essential questions you formulated for your app concept.
- **Challenge Creation** — Share the challenge statement derived from your essential questions.
- **Domain Investigation** — Discuss the research methods you used to delve deeper into the challenge.
- **Domain Personae** — Share your domain personae.
- **Problem/Opportunity Statement** — Present the clear problem/opportunity statement you crafted.
- **Solution Concepts** — Highlight the innovative solution concepts you generated using Mind Mapping.
- **Selecting ONE Solution Concept** — Explain your decision matrix and the chosen solution concept.
- **First Prototype: Create UI Screens (Lo-Fi)** — Showcase your initial app interface sketches.

### 2. Presentation

Your video should be well-structured, clear, and engaging. Use visuals, graphics, and narration to effectively convey the information.

### 3. Time Limit

The video should **not exceed 2 minutes** in length. Concisely explain each stage while maintaining clarity.

### 4. Submission

Submit your video via **Canvas**.

Your video will be evaluated based on the following criteria:
